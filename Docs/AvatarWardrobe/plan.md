# Approved direction: Option 03 — USC Locker

User selected USC Locker on 25 November and explicitly rejected the first crude procedural preview. The attached reference is authoritative: rounded toy-like characters, soft lighting, cardinal #99001B, gold #FFCC32, charcoal #171B22 and cream #F0E6D4. Use Barlow Black headings and Barlow Bold actions.

Native Unity controls sit above extracted approved locker-room art. Four separate transparent character assets show Classic, Varsity Jacket, Cardinal Hoodie and Sport Jacket. The selected card gets a gold border, checkmark and cardinal caption. Clicking a card previews its outfit; Equip writes a validated local preference. Back discards draft changes. Narrow windows use two columns.

The actual 3D Spirit receives the same outfit identity: varsity SC lettering/cream sleeves, cardinal hoodie and navy sport jacket. Its approved larger head and shorter, rounded limbs retain the original total scale, collider, gait, abilities, health and NPC uniforms. Selection applies again on scene load and after ejection. Menu art uses project assets derived from the approved reference; it does not replace gameplay with a flat screenshot.
