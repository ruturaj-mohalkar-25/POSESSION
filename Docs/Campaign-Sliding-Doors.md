# Campaign sliding doors

Levels 1 and 2 now present target-locked Guard puzzle doors using the same two-panel opening animation as Warehouse (Level 3). The old solid-colour door slab and padlock model are hidden. All seven campaign doorways use the Sci-Fi Security design: pale beveled panels, cyan angled light strips, a split geometric centre lock and a lighter frame. Both faces are styled for approaches from either room. Each doorway retains two sliding leaves and one signal lamp per required target. Lamps turn green as their own targets are hit; both leaves slide into the sides over 0.4 seconds when the last required target is hit. Thin-wall doors clip the visible panels to the frame while retracting, so leaves do not stick into empty space or neighbouring rooms.

The shared `SlidingDoorView` handles all three stages. Warehouse's existing component and saved references remain compatible through a thin subclass, retaining its authored geometry and group colours. The original shared `Door` controls unlocking, collision and the single `DoorOpened` event. Target requirements, exit conditions, breakable barriers, room sizes and actor settings are retained. Raised panels and lock halves are children of the moving leaves; decorative parts retract with them and add no colliders. Animation follows game time and stops when paused. Retry restores the closed door and unhit targets.

Stop Play Mode, reopen MainMenu, then play Levels 1 and 2. Shoot the door's required target(s) using a possessed Guard. Check that its lamps turn green, both panels visibly retract and the passage is clear. Retry to reset the puzzle. Level 3 uses the same animation as before.

The campaign builders apply this presentation through their existing final combat/profile pass. To update saved scenes without rebuilding rooms, use **Possession → Level Design → Style Campaign Security Doors**. The earlier **Sync Campaign Sliding Doors** command also reapplies the selected style. This pass rebinds each door to its own targets, including doors cloned by the Hunted builder, and replaces previous presentation parts rather than duplicating them.
