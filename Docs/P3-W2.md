Level_02's Heavy starts at the north-room marker (0, 23). Level_03's Heavies start at (8, 34) and (8, 18), on the approach side of their respective walls. Each has two scene-owned waypoints outside its body hierarchy. All use the shared Heavy prefab, SmashAbility, EnemyContactDamage and W0 indicator. The shared damage threshold keeps Guard bullets from breaking Heavy walls.

Two P4 wall segments crossed Level_03's right corridor at z=22 and z=14. Their widths are corrected in both the builder and saved scene so the intended U route can reach both Heavy walls and the exit. This prerequisite fix is a separate commit.

## Test in Unity

Use Unity 6000.3.22f1. Open `Assets/Scenes/Level_02.unity` or `Level_03.unity` and press Play.

P1's Guards are still spawn markers in the current integration. For a focused P3 test, select **Possession → P3 → Testing → Unlock Guard Door (Play Mode)**. This hits the targets only for the current play session; it does not save an unlocked door.

- Level_02: go through the unlocked door into the north room, approach the orange Heavy and press E. Move toward its wall, face north and left-click to smash. Eject and enter the exit as Spirit.
- Level_03: follow the left corridor north, cross the top, then turn south into the right corridor. Possess the first Heavy and face south to smash wall 1. Continue south to the second Heavy and repeat for wall 2. Eject before entering the exit.
- Inspect each Heavy's inherited Move Speed and Max HP fields outside Play mode. Damage, ability uses, possession and hostility follow the shared systems.

To restore P3 actors after a P4 scene rebuild, stop Play mode and run **Possession → P3 → W2 → Prepare Heavies in Levels 01-03**. This configures existing scenes without rebuilding geometry or HUD. Running it again updates the same actors and waypoint objects.

W3's exhaustive ejection and full-game QA across all four levels remain separate tasks. Automated W2 checks exercise controlled placements and puzzle events; the complete Guard-to-Heavy player route depends on P1's Guard placement and a manual playtest.

## Validation

All 15 focused native Unity PlayMode tests passed (7 W2 checks and 8 W0 indicator regressions). A separate Editor check reapplied setup twice in each level and verified that the same Heavy actors, patrol points and HUD were retained. Existing Level_01 scene data did not need changing because its Heavy already has the required stats.
